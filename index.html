Auto-Backup

Your backup folder is remembered across sessions. If the browser can't silently reconnect (e.g., after clearing site data), the app will auto-download a backup file at this interval as a safety net. Look for it in your browser's default Downloads folder, named scripture_memory_backup_YYYY-MM-DD.json.

How to Use

Getting Started

Go to the Verses tab to add your first verse. Type a reference (e.g., "John 3:16"), pick a translation, and click Look Up to auto-fill the text. Or type the verse text manually. Click Add Verse to save it.

Daily Practice

The Practice tab shows today's verses. Use Hide Text to test your recall, Test Yourself to type the verse, or Recite to speak it aloud and check your accuracy. Tap Next to move through your verses.

Marking Reviews

After practicing a verse, tap Mark Reviewed to record your completion. You can only mark one review per verse per day. The progress bar below each verse shows how many reviews you've completed toward promotion.

Spaced Repetition

Verses progress through phases as you review them consistently:

  • Daily — Practice every day. After 49 reviews and 49 days, promotes to weekly.
  • Weekly — Practice once a week on an assigned day. After 28 reviews and 28 weeks, promotes to monthly.
  • Monthly — Practice once a month on an assigned day. After 84 reviews and 84 months, the verse retires.
  • Retired — Fully memorized. The verse no longer appears in practice.

Both the required time and the required number of reviews must be met before a verse promotes.

Review History

Tap any verse in the Verses or Stats tab to open its detail view. Here you can see the full review history, add a review for a past date, or delete an accidental entry.

Verse Lookup

On the Verses tab, type a reference and choose a translation (CSB, NIV, or NKJV), then click Look Up. The verse text will auto-fill. You can edit it before adding if needed.

Backup

Your verses are saved in your browser automatically. For extra safety:

  • Folder sync (Chrome/Edge) — Set a backup folder (like OneDrive or Google Drive) and the app saves there on every change.
  • Scheduled download — If no folder is connected, the app auto-downloads a backup file on a weekly or monthly schedule.
  • Manual backup — Use Export/Import on the Backup tab anytime.

Dark Mode

Tap the sun/moon icon in the top-right corner to toggle between light and dark themes. Your preference is saved automatically.
